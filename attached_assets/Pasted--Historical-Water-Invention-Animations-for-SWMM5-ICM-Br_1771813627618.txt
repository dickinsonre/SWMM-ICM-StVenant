# Historical Water Invention Animations for SWMM5 & ICM

## Bringing 300,000 Years of Water Engineering to Life Through Interactive Hydraulic Simulations

---

## The Concept

Each animation takes a real historical water invention from your database and shows it operating as a **live hydraulic simulation** — with the SWMM5/ICM equations visible, the flow animated, and the engineering principles highlighted. The user can adjust parameters and watch the ancient system respond.

These are not just pretty pictures. These are **teaching tools** that connect ancient engineering intuition to modern computational hydraulics.
