**The animation behavior:**

**Phase 1 — Free Flow (depth < 50%):**
- Water flows through the chamber with visible velocity vectors
- Entry and exit head loss arrows pulse proportional to magnitude
- The water surface is slightly lower at the outlet than the inlet (showing head loss)
- Benching channels are visible guiding the flow

**Phase 2 — Rising (depth 50-100%):**
- Water level rises in the shaft
- Entry/exit head losses increase visibly
- Velocity vectors slow as cross-section area increases in the shaft
- The energy grade line and hydraulic grade line diverge

**Phase 3 — Surcharge (depth > 100%):**
- Water fills the shaft completely
- A "pressurized" indicator appears
- The Preissmann slot activates (for ICM) with a visual narrow extension above the cover
- Pressure head is shown above the shaft cover

**Phase 4 — Flooding (depth > shaft height):**
- Water overflows the shaft
- A surface flood pool appears at ground level
- Flood volume counter starts incrementing
- The ponded area geometry activates (if defined)

### 🎬 Animation 12: Orifice Discharge Coefficient Conversion

```
ORIFICE ANIMATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

   SWMM5:                    ICM:
   Q = Cd × A × √(2gH)      Q = Cd_ICM × A × √(2gH)
```
