1.  **Real-World Application:** It directly connects the previously discussed solver behavior and headloss models to a **common, high-stakes modeling challenge**: accurately representing street-level flow entry into the sewer network. 3.  **Illustrates a Key Solver Interface Point:** Inlets are a primary point of interaction between **overland flow (2D or 1D overland)** and the **pressurized/1D sewer network**. 4.  **Complements the Headloss Guide:** While the headloss guide focuses on *internal* junction losses, the inlet guide focuses on the *external* transition from surface to sewer. Together, they provide a complete picture of energy loss modeling at connections.

### **Proposal for Integration: "Modeling Surface Transitions" Module**

I recommend adding this as a **third major section**, alongside the existing "Solver Architecture" and "Headloss" sections. This would transform the app's scope from a **core theory comparison** into a **comprehensive practical guide**.

**Suggested Title:**`Surface-to-Sewer Transitions: Inlets & Culverts`

**Content Structure:**

1.  **Introduction:** Explain why inlet representation is critical for urban flood modeling.
2.  **The Inlet Element:** A diagram showing how ICM conceptually splits a manhole into an above-ground ("flooding") and below-ground ("sewer") element, linked by an inlet with a defined capacity.
3.  **Inlet Methods (Interactive Chooser):**
    *   **Head/Discharge Tables:** For known rating curves (like the previous gully example).
    *   **HEC-22 Equations:**
        *   **Continuous Curb/Grate:** For flow *past* an inlet. Show the efficiency calculation`E=f(L, Q, Sx)`.
        *   **SAG Curb/Grate/Combination:** For inlets at low points. Show the transition from weir flow (`Q ∝ d^(3/2)`) to orifice flow (`Q ∝ sqrt(d)`).
    *   **Flow-Efficiency Tables:** For UK or other regional standards (e.g., "Spacing of Road Gullies").
    *   **User-Defined Equations:**`Q = a * d^b`.
4.  **Interactive Diagram: "The Inlet During a Storm"**
    *   **Visual:** A side-view cross-section of a street gutter and an inlet leading to a manhole.
    *   **Controls:**
        *   Inlet Type selector (Curb, Grate, Combination).
        *   Gutter slope (`Sx`), flow (`Q`), and inlet length (`L`) sliders.
        *   A toggle between`Limit of Inflow` (street flooding) and`Limit of Outflow` (sewer surcharge) scenarios.
    *   **Animation:** As the street flow increases, the diagram shows the depth of spread (`T`), the calculated inlet efficiency or captured flow, and the bypass flow that continues down the street. For the`Limit of Outflow` scenario, it shows water backing up from the manhole through the inlet.
    *   **Educational Insight:** Makes abstract HEC-22 equations tangible. Shows how inlet type and street geometry determine capture efficiency, which directly impacts surface flooding results and sanitary sewer overflow (SSO) predictions.
